# SHEKU Target Organizations and Industries

SHEKU is designed for organizations where knowledge is distributed across documents, systems and operational teams and where traceability, governance and reliable retrieval matter.

## Primary industry profiles

Relevant sectors include, without limiting the product architecture to them:

- energy and utilities;
- mining and natural resources;
- manufacturing;
- infrastructure and engineering;
- transportation and logistics;
- industrial services;
- other asset-intensive or regulated organizations.

SHEKU must not encode any of these sectors as fixed domain models. Industry-specific terminology, taxonomies, workflows and connectors belong in configurable templates or adapters.

## Common product problems

SHEKU targets recurring enterprise problems such as:

- fragmented documents and knowledge sources;
- difficulty identifying authoritative or current information;
- weak provenance and lineage between source information and derived knowledge;
- slow enterprise search and manual information discovery;
- inconsistent document and knowledge governance;
- disconnected systems and duplicated information;
- limited auditability of processing, search or assistant behavior;
- dependence on individual experts for organizational knowledge;
- AI initiatives that lack a governed, reusable knowledge foundation.

## Positioning rule

The product is industrially oriented but remains generic. A sector, plant, asset, department, regulatory framework or customer workflow must never become mandatory SHEKU architecture.
